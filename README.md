# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

An interactive HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze employee attrition patterns and identify key HR insights.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand attrition trends across different departments, job roles, genders, and age groups.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of important HR metrics and helps identify areas where employee retention strategies may be required.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition.
- Analyze attrition across different job roles.
- Compare attrition by gender.
- Analyze attrition across different age groups.
- Monitor key employee and HR metrics.
- Provide actionable HR insights and recommendations.

## 📌 Key KPIs

- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Attrition Count:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
- **Average Age:** 37 years
- **Average Monthly Income:** 6.50K

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Interactive slicers for:
  - Department
  - Gender
  - Job Role
  - Education Field
- Key HR Insights
- HR Recommendations

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Overall employee attrition rate is **16.1%**.
- **237 employees** have left the organization out of **1,470 employees**.
- Research & Development has the highest attrition count among the departments.
- Attrition varies across different job roles and age groups.
- The dashboard helps identify employee segments that may require further HR analysis.

## 💡 HR Recommendations

- Focus on employee retention strategies in departments with higher attrition.
- Analyze factors contributing to attrition in high-risk job roles.
- Conduct employee engagement and satisfaction surveys.
- Review career growth and development opportunities.
- Use regular HR analytics to monitor changes in employee attrition.
